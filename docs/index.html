Privacy Policy

Rolling Dice

Last updated: August 18, 2026

In short: Rolling Dice collects no data, connects to no servers, and shares nothing with anyone. It's a fully offline dice-rolling app.

What data we collect

None. Rolling Dice does not collect, store on any server, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or analytics. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no network requests of any kind — the app works identically with your device in airplane mode.

Roll history

The app keeps a history of your recent dice rolls so you can look back at them. This history is stored only in your device's local app storage, never leaves your device, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Motion sensor (shake-to-roll)

Rolling Dice reads your device's motion sensor to detect when you shake your phone, so you can roll the dice with a shake instead of tapping a button. This sensor data is used instantly, on-device, purely to detect the gesture — it is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Permissions

  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback when you roll. Does not access or share any information.
  • Motion sensors — used only for the shake-to-roll gesture, as described above.

Third parties

Rolling Dice does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting services, or any other third-party service. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no data from any user, it does not collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that uses the network — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the Rolling Dice Google Play Store listing.

Rolling Dice — this policy applies to the Android app published at com.agenticsamir.rollingdice.
