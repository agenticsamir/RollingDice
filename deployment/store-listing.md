## Full description (max 4000 characters)
```
Rolling Dice is a simple, satisfying dice roller that looks and feels like the real thing.

Roll 1 to 6 dice at once and watch each one tumble into place with a realistic, physical-feeling animation — complete with shading, a bouncing drop shadow, and a dice-clatter sound effect. Feel every roll with haptic feedback, or just give your phone a shake to roll instead of tapping a button.

FEATURES
• Roll 1–6 dice at once, with the total automatically added up
• Realistic tumbling animation for every die, not flat static icons
• Shake-to-roll — physically shake your phone to roll
• Sound effect and haptic feedback on every roll
• Roll history, saved on your device so you can look back at past rolls
• Clean, distraction-free single screen — no menus, no setup

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Rolling Dice works completely offline. There are no accounts, no ads, no trackers, and no data ever leaves your device — see the in-app Privacy Policy link for details.

Perfect for board games, tabletop RPGs, classroom activities, or settling any argument that needs a fair roll of the dice.
```
